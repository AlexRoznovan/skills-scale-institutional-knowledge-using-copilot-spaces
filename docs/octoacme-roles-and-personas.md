# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA and Testing Leads define quality standards, test strategy, and acceptance criteria validation. They partner with Developers, Product Managers, and Project Managers to reduce risk before release and ensure the delivered solution meets user and business needs.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, quality gates, and acceptance validation steps
- Review user stories and acceptance criteria for testability
- Manage manual and automated test execution for critical flows
- Identify defects, prioritize fixes, and track release quality
- Partner with Developers on test coverage and regression protection
- Escalate quality risks and support release decisions

### Goals
- Reduce defects that reach production
- Improve confidence in each release
- Ensure the product meets agreed quality and usability expectations

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and backlog refinement for testability
- Defect triage and release readiness reviews
- Status updates on quality risks, coverage, and product health

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works closely with Developers to validate implementation quality and regression risk
- Aligns with Product Managers on acceptance criteria and user impact
- Reports dependencies and risks to Project Managers for planning and release coordination

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters or Agile Coaches help the team work effectively, improve delivery flow, and remove impediments. They support healthy ceremonies and help the team improve collaboration, transparency, and accountability over time.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, reviews, retrospectives, and standups
- Identify delivery bottlenecks and help remove blockers
- Coach the team on Agile practices, working agreements, and continuous improvement
- Support cross-team communication and decision-making cadence
- Help Product Managers and Project Managers maintain a clear and realistic delivery rhythm

### Goals
- Improve team effectiveness and delivery predictability
- Maintain a sustainable and collaborative working environment
- Strengthen continuous learning and self-organization within the team

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- Team retrospectives and improvement planning
- Agile coaching conversations with project leads and delivery teams

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Project Managers on sprint health, dependencies, and risk escalation
- Supports Developers by helping maintain focus, reducing friction, and improving workflow
- Partners with Product Managers to keep priorities realistic and backlog refinement effective

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects provide technical direction, design guidance, and engineering standards. They ensure the solution is scalable, maintainable, and aligned with the project goals and broader platform strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Review technical designs and major implementation decisions
- Define architectural patterns, standards, and technical guardrails
- Help evaluate trade-offs between speed, quality, and maintainability
- Mentor Developers and support technical decision-making
- Identify architecture risks, technical debt, and dependency constraints

### Goals
- Deliver a robust, maintainable, and scalable technical solution
- Reduce rework and avoid avoidable implementation churn
- Enable the team to build with confidence and consistency

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and architecture discussions
- Technical risk reviews and trade-off analysis
- Code review feedback and implementation guidance

### Interaction with existing roles
- Collaborates with Developers to turn product goals into feasible technical approaches
- Advises Product Managers and Project Managers on technical feasibility, timeline, and risk
- Helps QA/Testing Lead understand critical system boundaries and areas of higher regression risk

---

## Designer / UX Lead

### Role Summary
Designers and UX Leads ensure the product is usable, accessible, and aligned with customer needs. They translate product goals into clear user experiences and help the team design solutions that are intuitive and valuable.

### Responsibilities
- Define user flows, interaction patterns, and interface conventions
- Partner with Product Managers on customer problems and opportunity framing
- Create or validate design assets, prototypes, and usability decisions
- Ensure accessibility, consistency, and good user experience standards
- Support Developers and QA in validating behavior against intended design

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, inclusive, and customer-centered experiences
- Improve adoption, usability, and business value of the product
- Reduce ambiguity between product intent and implementation

### Typical Communication
- Product discovery, backlog refinement, and design review sessions
- Usability feedback loops and prototype walkthroughs
- Design handoff and implementation alignment with Developers

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Product Managers to shape user outcomes and success criteria
- Provides design guidance to Developers and QA to ensure implementation matches expected behavior
- Keeps Project Managers informed of design dependencies, risks, and milestones

---

## Sponsor / Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Sponsors and key stakeholders provide business direction, funding, and strategic alignment. They ensure projects remain connected to organizational goals, required outcomes, and resource support.

### Responsibilities
- Approve the project charter, scope, and key decisions
- Provide business context, success expectations, and priority alignment
- Allocate budget, people, and executive support when needed
- Help resolve cross-functional conflicts and business trade-offs
- Review progress at key milestones and support escalation when needed

### Goals
- Ensure the project delivers measurable value to the organization
- Protect strategic alignment and business continuity
- Enable the team to succeed through sponsorship and decision support

### Typical Communication
- Milestone reviews and governance updates
- Decision points, approvals, and escalations
- Weekly or monthly stakeholder updates and strategic check-ins

### Interaction with existing roles
- Partners with Product Managers to confirm business priorities and success measures
- Provides strategic guidance and resource support to Project Managers
- Collaborates with QA and Delivery leads when changes affect scope, quality, or release readiness

---

## DevOps / Release Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps and Release Engineers make delivery reliable and repeatable. They manage environments, deployment automation, and release readiness so teams can ship confidently and recover quickly when incidents occur.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain CI/CD pipelines, deployment automation, and environment health
- Support staging and production release processes
- Monitor operational readiness and performance of deployment workflows
- Partner with Developers on environment configuration and release dependencies
- Help define rollback plans and incident response readiness

### Goals
- Increase delivery speed without reducing reliability
- Improve release consistency and observability
- Minimize operational risk during deployment and recovery

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and deployment readiness reviews
- Incident communication and rollback coordination
- CI/CD health updates and release status reporting

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Developers to ensure code can be deployed safely and predictably
- Partners with QA/Testing Lead on release validation and smoke testing
- Coordinates with Project Managers and Sponsors on deployment windows, risks, and release communications

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- When a team is planning work, these roles clarify ownership, dependencies, and communication channels to support better outcomes.
